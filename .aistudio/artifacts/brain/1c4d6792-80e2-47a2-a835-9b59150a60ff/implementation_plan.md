### 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Bridges the gap between Western calorie-tracking apps (which fail miserably at Indian household cooking dynamics) and the lived reality of Indian eating habits. It turns guilt into laughter, providing real macro tracking while normalizing family pressure, street food urges, and social dining.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: Urban Indian millennials and Gen-Z (ages 21–38) who want to stay fit, build muscle, or manage weight, but live in environments rich in emotional food pressure (moms serving 3 extra rotis with ghee, office samosa parties, late-night Maggi cravings, and 5-day wedding marathons).
- **Key Value**: Eradicates diet shame. Instead of passive-aggressive red danger bars, it offers witty, relatable validation, actionable damage control, and tangible 3D spatial macro visualization without compromising nutritional truth.

### 2. User Experience & Visual Design

#### A. Tone of Voice & Personality Guidelines
1. **The Persona: "The Triad Coach"**:
   - **40% Loving Desi Mom**: Demands you eat your dal, worries if you didn't drink enough water, but will emotionally blackmail you into sleeping on time.
   - **35% Sarcastic Gym Trainer**: Calls out pseudo-fitness myths (*"Green tea does not cancel out 4 cheese naan, beta"*), questions your 15-minute warmup that was actually 14 minutes of reels.
   - **25% Supportive Best Friend**: Never judges the midnight cheat meal, immediately helps you calculate the recovery plan without starvation.
2. **Humor Style & Cultural Anchors**:
   - *Tinda vs Paneer*: Celebrating culinary compromises without suffering.
   - *The "Just One Bite" Jalebi Trap*: Acknowledging that Indian sweets defy the laws of thermodynamics.
   - *Rishtedaar Radar*: Preparing comebacks for aunties commenting on weight gains or losses.
   - *The Aadhaar Card Weight Reality Check*: Acknowledging optimism vs digital reality.
3. **The Empathy-Data Balance**:
   - Serious, clinical macro math (Protein, Carbs, Fats, Dietary Fiber, Sodium) displayed in crisp tabular typography.
   - Instant humorous softening: If a user logs a 1,200 kcal Biryani binge, the app doesn't flash red alarms; it delivers a chuckle (*"Arre Biryani lover, the onion raita counts as a vegetable salad only in dreams. Drink 500ml water and let's balance tomorrow's breakfast."*).

### 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Zero External 3D Assets (100% Procedural Three.js Meshes)**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Construct the Indian Thali, brass katoris, rotis, and samosa via procedural Three.js BufferGeometry (`CylinderGeometry`, `LatheGeometry`, `ExtrudeGeometry`) and procedural canvas textures.
  - *Why*: Eliminates broken GLTF 404s, loads instantaneously with zero download wait time, and runs at 60fps across all devices.
- **Decision 2: Seamless Tabbed Architecture with Collapsible 3D Bottom Dock**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Allow toggling between the 3D Nutrition Studio, Food Tracker & Wedding Mode, Blueprint & Copy Guidelines, AI Coach Chat, and Meal Kit Store.
  - *Why*: Delivers an unforgettable, publishable showcase combining 3D spatial tech with comedy and product design depth.
